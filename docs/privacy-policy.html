StarFleet Idle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 May 2025

Introduction

StarFleet Idle ("the Game") is developed by Roy Hillis and Abbie Hillis. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our Game.

Information We Collect

The Game itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All game progress is stored locally on your device.

Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data to serve relevant ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this data.

In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Storage

All game data (progress, settings, achievements) is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal data.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
