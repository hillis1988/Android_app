StarFleet Idle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 May 2026

Introduction

StarFleet Idle ("the Game") is developed by Roy Hillis and Abbie Hillis. This privacy policy explains how the Game and its third-party services collect and use information.

Information We Collect Directly

The Game itself does not directly collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information. All gameplay progress is stored locally on your device.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

StarFleet Idle integrates with the following Google services. These services may collect information independently:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The Game displays optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App interaction data for ad targeting

For more information, see Google's Ads Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

Optional in-app purchases (gem packs, developer tips) are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store any payment information. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Google Play Games Services (Leaderboards)

The Game integrates with Play Games Services to display leaderboards. If you choose to sign in:

  • Your Play Games display name is shown alongside your scores
  • Your scores are stored on Google's servers
  • Sign-in is optional and can be skipped

Children's Privacy

The Game is rated for ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

EU/UK Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, AdMob will request your consent for personalised advertising via a Google-provided consent dialog when the app first runs. You can change your consent preferences at any time.

Local Data Storage

Game progress (ships, credits, achievements, settings) is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data does not leave your device unless you sign in to Play Games Services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
